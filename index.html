New to Raidsmith? How it works

How it works
1

Import

Paste a Raid-Helper link or event ID, drop in the JSON, or reopen a roster you saved.

2

Optimize

Import arranges groups automatically, except that an event whose Raid-Helper groups are already set keeps them. Press Optimize after any change to rebuild your groups around the buffs that matter most. Too many sign-ups? The strongest raid gets seated and the rest wait on the bench.

3

Share

Check your buff coverage, drag anyone you want moved, then use Share: Copy for MRT for the in-game groups, or Copy live link to send the plan.

For guilds

Raidsmith also keeps your guild's records: one place for your roster, raids, attendance and loot, with the planner built in.

  • Roster

    People, characters and alts, brought in from Raid-Helper, Warcraft Logs or Battle.net.

  • Raids

    A weekly schedule, sign-ups and a published line-up for every raid.

  • Attendance

    Worked out from your Warcraft Logs reports, under the rules your guild sets.

  • Loot

    Gargul exports, who won what, and how the loot is spread.

  • Planner

    Raid groups built from any raid's sign-ups, saved with the raid.

Sign in with Discord To start a guild you need to own or administer its Discord server. The planner above works without signing in.
