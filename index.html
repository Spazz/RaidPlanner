Frequently asked questions
Is Party Planner free?

Yes. There is no account, no login and no paid tier. Everything runs in your browser.

Where does my roster data go?

Nowhere. Sign-ups are fetched by your browser directly from Raid-Helper and never touch our server. Saved rosters live in your browser's local storage, and share links carry the layout in the URL fragment, which browsers never send to the host.

How does it decide who goes where?

Each party is scored by the buffs its members receive, so melee end up with Windfury, casters with their totems and auras, and tanks with their healers. You can pick Max DPS, Tank Mitigation or Balanced, and drag anyone afterwards.

What happens when more people sign up than the raid holds?

You always keep enough tanks and healers. The remaining seats go to whoever adds the most, and everyone else waits on the bench, where you can swap them in with one click.

Loading from URL failed. What now?

Some browsers or network setups block direct requests to Raid-Helper. Open the API link (raid-helper.dev/api/raidplan/<id>) in a new tab, copy the JSON, and use the Paste JSON tab.

Does it support 10-man raids?

Yes. Pick Karazhan or Zul'Aman from the raid dropdown and the planner switches to two groups with 10-man tank and healer floors.

How do I get the layout to my raid?

Click Share to copy a link that carries the whole layout, or use Export to download it as JSON.
