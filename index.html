Frequently asked questions
Is Party Planner free?

Yes. There is no account, no login and no paid tier. Everything runs in your browser.

Where does my roster data go?

Sign-ups are fetched by your browser directly from Raid-Helper and never touch our server. Named saves and your latest 30 imports live in this browser's local storage. Working plans save your latest changes automatically, while import history keeps the original snapshots. Clearing site data removes both saves and history. Share links carry the layout in the URL fragment, which browsers never send to the host.

How does it decide who goes where?

Each party is scored by the buffs its members receive, so melee end up with Windfury, casters with their totems and auras, and tanks with their healers. You can pick Max DPS, Tank Mitigation or Balanced, and drag anyone afterwards.

What happens when more people sign up than the raid holds?

You always keep enough tanks and healers. The remaining seats go to whoever adds the most, and everyone else waits on the bench, where you can swap them in with one click.

Loading from URL failed. What now?

Some browsers or network setups block direct requests to Raid-Helper. Open the API link (raid-helper.dev/api/raidplan/<id>) in a new tab, copy the JSON, and paste the JSON into the import box.

People signed up after I built the groups. Do I start over?

No. A roster imported from a Raid-Helper link remembers its event. When you reopen a saved roster, the planner checks the event and shows what changed. Click Refresh (in the toolbar or on the banner) to add new sign-ups to the bench, remove anyone who withdrew, and update changed specs, while everyone else keeps their seat.

Where do Bench, Tentative, Late and Absent sign-ups go?

Into the Sign-ups panel under the groups, one tab per Raid-Helper status. Only confirmed sign-ups are seated automatically. Drag anyone from Bench, Tentative or Late into a group when you want them; Absent players can be seated too, after you confirm (and pick a class if they didn't). On Refresh, people move between tabs as their Raid-Helper status changes.

Does it support 10-man raids?

Yes. Pick Karazhan or Zul'Aman from the raid dropdown and the planner switches to two groups with 10-man tank and healer floors.

Does it support Classic Era?

Yes. Molten Core, Onyxia's Lair, Blackwing Lair, Zul'Gurub, Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, Temple of Ahn'Qiraj and Naxxramas all have real Classic Era buff, debuff and optimizer rules, and the planner is faction-aware — Shaman totems for Horde, Paladin auras for Alliance.

Does it support WoW Forever?

Yes, using confirmed Forever beta data. Barrow Deeps (10), Hyjal Summit (20) and Onyxia's Lair (40) are modeled with real rules — and unlike Classic Era, Forever has no faction lock: the new race/class combos put Paladin and Shaman on both factions, so a raid can freely mix totems and auras. Trueshot Aura is baseline for every Hunter spec, and Leader of the Pack and Moonkin Aura share one exclusive crit buff per group, so Feral and Balance druids spread out instead of stacking.

How do I get the layout to my raid?

Click Share to copy a link that carries the whole layout, or use Export to download it as JSON.

Can I load the groups into MRT (Method Raid Tools)?

Yes, on Classic Era and TBC — MRT ships maintained builds for both, and its Raid Groups module isn't restricted to Retail. Export > Copy MRT String, then in game open MRT > Raid Groups > Import, pick the From ExRT export string tab, paste, and give the profile a name. MRT can then move everyone into the right party for you. WoW Forever support is unconfirmed — MRT has no Forever build yet since Forever is still in beta.

Can I paste a roster that isn't from Raid-Helper?

Yes. The import box also accepts a plain list — one player per line or comma-separated, in any of "Name Class Spec", "Name - Spec Class", a Discord-style "@Name (Spec Class)", a WoW /who-style "Name Level 60 Race Class" line, or CSV with an optional header row. You'll see a preview of what was recognized, with anything ambiguous flagged for review and unrecognized lines listed separately, before anything is imported.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes — press ? anywhere in the planner for the full list (Optimize, Undo/Redo, Save, Share/Export, and switching between Plan and Assignments).

Can I back up my data, or move it to another browser or device?

Yes. Open the gear icon (Settings and data) and click Export data backup to download every saved roster, comp template, working plan and import history record kept in this browser as one JSON file, with a timestamp and version header. On the new browser, open the same gear icon and click Import data backup, pick that file, and choose Merge (adds anything missing and keeps whichever copy of a shared item is newer — the default) or Replace (overwrites matching data with the backup). You'll see a summary of exactly what will change before anything is written, and a file that doesn't look like a Party Planner backup is rejected without touching your existing data.

I dismissed a feature tip and want to see it again

Party Planner shows a few one-time callouts pointing at easy-to-miss features (Assignments, the toolbar menus, the player editor, keyboard shortcuts). Once shown, each one stays dismissed.
